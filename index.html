Admission Process

Your journey toward academic excellence starts here. We make our enrollment process as simple and transparent as possible for families. Follow these four straightforward steps to secure your student's place.


Step 1: Application

Complete the online application form with accurate information about your student.


Step 2: Documentation

Submit all required documents, including transcripts, identification, and any other necessary paperwork


Step 3: Interview

Attend the scheduled interview with our admissions committee to discuss your student's qualifications and interests.


Step 4: Decision

We will notify you of our decision within two weeks of receiving your application.


Step 5: Enrollment

Once accepted, complete the enrollment process by submitting any additional forms and fees required to secure your student's place at SteadyLearners.
